# title:       Sheikh Zayed's Chrysler Is Back In Abu Dhabi
# description: The car Sheikh Zayed was filmed driving in the documentary Farewell Arabia has been recreated for the new Zayed National Museum. A 1966 Chrysler Newport in Formal Black, sourced from California and rebuilt to match — flag mount, oversized desert wheels and all. It sits on sand, with tyre tracks laid behind it. All photographs shot by me at the museum, Saadiyat Cultural District. Sources: Gulf News, The National.
